05
Your data, answered

Questions about
your data.

The questions we hear most before someone signs up — answered plainly.

Where is my data stored?

In a managed Postgres database hosted in Sydney, Australia. The app itself is served from a global network for speed, but every client record, booking, and financial entry you create lives in that Sydney-region database.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and data is encrypted at rest. Each business's data is isolated from every other business on the platform — there's no shared access between accounts.

Who can actually see my data?

Only you and anyone you invite into your account. We don't sell or share your business data with third parties, and our team doesn't look at your data unless you contact us for support and ask us to.

How long do you keep it on file?

For as long as your account stays active. If you ever cancel, we hold your data for a short window in case you want to export it or come back — after that it's permanently deleted from our systems.
